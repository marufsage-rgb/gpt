# Oman AI Case Guardian v2 — MarufEdge

Production-oriented multilingual legal research + evidence ERP starter.

Core principle:
AI is a research/detection assistant, not a lawyer or court. Every legal finding must carry source, version/date, evidence reference, quote, confidence and human-review status.

Primary official Oman references currently indexed:
- Royal Decree 53/2023 Labour Law — Ministry of Labour official portal.
- Wage Protection System (WPS) — Ministry of Labour official resources.
- Ministerial Decision 602/2025 and later amendments — verify current official text before relying on it.
- Ministry Labour Complaints service information.
